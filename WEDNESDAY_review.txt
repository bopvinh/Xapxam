# WEDNESDAY's Ergonomics Review (Stage 2)

Objective: Ensure gameplay comfort.

- Visual Hierarchy: Board must be the primary focus.
- Keyboard: Accessible, readable keys, no overlap with grid.
- Interactions: Animations must be performant, no UI blocking.

Ergonomics Criteria:
- No clipped 'P' or 'Q' keys.
- Grid must not be obscured.
- Touch-friendly sizing on mobile.
